Before you begin

Set up your
environment.

What makes Palette different from every other AI color tool is photo quality. These conditions directly determine accuracy.

☀️ Natural daylight only Critical

Sit directly facing a window with soft, indirect daylight. No direct sun, no lamps, no ring lights — artificial light shifts undertones and will corrupt your result.

📷 No filters, no beauty mode Critical

Open your plain camera app and turn off all enhancements. Beauty mode, portrait mode, and filters alter skin tone data in ways that break the analysis.

💄 Minimal or no makeup

Go as bare as you're comfortable. Foundation, concealer, and blush mask the undertone signals the analysis relies on.
